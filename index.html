I am Minjae Kwen, a Ph.D. student in the Quantum Science and Engineering program at Harvard University. My research focuses on pulse-level control and state preparation in analog quantum simulators, and on quantum reservoir computing. I am also interested in quantum channels and open-system dynamics.

Research Interests
